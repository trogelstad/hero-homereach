Your VA Homebuying Advantage Plan

Zero down is powerful. Answer seven quick questions and see a planning summary built around your own numbers, your region and your priorities. No sign-up required to see your results.

Free · Educational · Not a lender · No pressure
1. Where in Colorado are you planning to buy? This helps us surface the assistance programs available in your area.
2. What price range are you considering? A planning range, not an exact number. This scales the examples in your plan.
3. Is this your first time using your VA loan benefit?
4. Do you currently receive VA disability compensation?
5. What do you currently have available for closing and moving costs? A planning range only. Nothing is verified or stored as a financial record.
6. What matters most to you right now? Choose as many as apply.
7. Have you already talked with a lender or real estate agent?
Your plan appears instantly below. No email required to see it.
Educational disclaimer: Hero HomeReach provides educational information only. Program availability, eligibility, approval, assistance amounts, loan terms and closing costs depend on program rules, credit, income, loan type, property, location, occupancy and other factors. A free Hero HomeReach consultation can help buyers understand which questions to ask and which paths may be worth reviewing.
